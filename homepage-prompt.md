## THE JOB

Rebuild the homepage at `web/src/app/page.tsx` so it reads as a bold, vibrant, professionally designed front door for Lucky Star Academy's school system — a page a parent in Yendi opens on a phone and immediately trusts, and a designer looks at and believes a person made. It is a portal gateway, not a brochure: its job is to get an administrator, a teacher or a pupil to the right sign-in confidently, to say in a few lines whose system this is, and to hand anyone looking for the school itself to the public website. It stays short — four movements: a hero, the three portals, a short "this school" section, and the footer.

The page is flat today and you should be able to name why before you fix it. Every section sits on the same pale ground inside the same `maxWidth="lg"` container; every surface is the same outlined white card with the same 20px radius; the hero gradient runs from off-white to a 16% tint of green, which on most screens is indistinguishable from white; the display headline is set at a size that barely outranks the body copy; nothing overlaps, nothing bleeds to the viewport edge, nothing is asymmetric; and the hero's right half is four empty frames, because `StudentPhotoCollage` points at `public/photos/*.jpg` files that do not exist. The "Get started" button leads to school registration, which the sign-in page itself says is already done, so the primary call to action is the wrong one. Fix the causes, not the symptoms.

Work in this order. First, write a short concept note, `web/HOMEPAGE.md`: the section-by-section plan with a rough wireframe in ASCII or a description, the hero treatment, where the deep-green and gold moments fall, what motion happens and where, the type sizes at desktop and phone, and how each of the "flat" causes above is answered. Note explicitly how the hero relates to the standing decision in `DESIGN.md` §8 (a brand gradient with subtle school motifs): you may evolve it toward a deep, saturated green ground with the motifs rendered in gold, but say so and wait for approval. Post the note and stop for a go-ahead. Then build, checking at desktop and 360px after every section, with the real photographs in place.

What a senior front-end developer would reach for here, to be confirmed in your note rather than copied blindly. Alternate grounds so no two adjacent sections share a background: a deep-green hero, a light portals section, a green or gold "this school" band, a deep-green footer. Let the type do the heavy lifting: the Fraunces display headline at a genuine display size — on the order of 64–88px at desktop, fluid down to about 40px on a phone — against Hanken Grotesk body copy, so the scale contrast alone creates hierarchy. Let the photographs break the grid: a collage that overlaps the hero's bottom edge into the next section, one frame tilted a degree or two, frames of different sizes, shadows tinted with the deep green rather than grey. Use gold as a bold accent for once — a solid gold label or sticker, a thick gold rule, large gold numerals 01 · 02 · 03 on the portal cards, the star motif drawn crisp rather than at 5% opacity — while keeping it off white as a text colour. Make the portal cards feel like three doors, not three tiles: distinct by number and colour treatment, with the whole card clickable, a visible focus ring, and a hover that lifts and shifts the arrow. Give the nav a life: transparent over the hero, becoming a blurred translucent bar with a hairline once the page scrolls. Add subtle, honest motion — sections rise and fade in as they enter the viewport, cards lift on hover, the headline's gold rule draws in once — all disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Add texture that machines skip: a faint grain over the deep-green sections, a hand-drawn underline path in SVG under the hero keyword, generous section padding of 96–128px at desktop so the page breathes. Make "Sign in" the one primary button, and demote school registration to a quiet text link in the footer.

## THE WHY

This page is the first thing every user of the system sees, every day, and the first thing the client sees when judging what ALBASA WEB delivered. The app behind it has been redesigned with care and the dashboards now tell the school things it needs to know; a flat, pale, half-empty front door undersells all of that. A parent who lands here on a phone at night decides in two seconds whether this is the school's real system or something a student built for a project. Bold and vibrant is not decoration for its own sake — the school's crest is a strong green and a bright gold, and the school's own photographs are of children drumming, playing football and working at computers; a page that renders that identity at 16% opacity is hiding it. The main public website is being built separately, so this page does not need to carry the school's story; it needs to carry its confidence.

## THE GUARDRAILS

Scope is `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/ui/StudentPhotoCollage.tsx`, `src/components/ui/SiteFooter.tsx`, any new components you create under `src/components/home/`, and small additive changes to `src/theme.ts` and `globals.css` (a grain utility, a display-size clamp). Do not touch the auth pages, the app shell, the portals, or anything under `src/lib/**` or `supabase/**`, and do not change or remove any route — `/register/school` still exists, it just stops being the hero's call to action.

No new dependencies. Motion is CSS transitions and a single small `"use client"` `Reveal` component built on `IntersectionObserver`; no framer-motion, GSAP, Lottie or particle libraries. `page.tsx` stays a server component; only the pieces that need browser APIs — the scroll-aware nav and the reveal wrapper — are client components, and they receive plain props. Every effect respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` by rendering the final state immediately. No autoplay video, no parallax that scrolls against the finger on a phone, no animated numbers, no marquee.

Imagery is the school's real photographs from `public/photos/` through `next/image` with `fill`, correct `sizes`, `priority` on the largest hero image only, and meaningful alt text on every one. If a listed file is missing when you build, stop and say so rather than substituting stock, an illustration or a generated image; the collage may be composed of fewer frames than planned but never of empty ones. Do not use the SchoolAI onboarding screenshots in `public/SchoolAI Web Onboarding/` as anything but a reference for polish; nothing on the page may resemble them.

No invented content. The page states only what is true and supplied: the school's name, Yendi and the Northern Region, Primary 1–6, the campus names, and whichever of the motto, founding year and website link were filled in above. No statistics, no testimonials, no "trusted by", no feature grid describing the software, no placeholder copy, no links to pages that do not exist. The footer keeps the credit exactly as it is — `Designed & Developed by AlbasaWEB` linking to `https://albasaweb.com` in a new tab — and gains only the school line, the year, the registration text link and the website link.

Stay inside the design system. Fraunces for display and headings, Hanken Grotesk for everything else; colours through theme tokens and the named constants (`BRAND_GOLD`, `BRAND_GREEN_DARK`, `PAGE_BG`, …), with any new colour — a saturated hero green, a gold tint — added as a named constant in `theme.ts`, not a hex in `sx`. Gold is never a text colour on a light ground; on the deep green, text is white or the warm off-white and passes 4.5:1. Update `DESIGN.md` §3 (the gold placements) and §8 (the hero) to match what you ship, in the same commit. Radii come from the scale in `DESIGN.md` §4; a tilted photo is a transform, not a new radius.

Performance and accessibility are part of "professional". The page must score 90 or above on Lighthouse Performance and 95 or above on Accessibility and Best Practices on mobile; largest contentful paint under 2.5 seconds on a throttled mobile profile; no cumulative layout shift from fonts, images or reveal animations (reserve space, animate opacity and transform only). Every interactive element has a visible focus state on the deep green as well as on white; the nav, buttons and portal cards work by keyboard; the page has one `h1`; headings descend in order; the grain overlay and decorative SVGs are `aria-hidden`. Nothing scrolls horizontally at 360px, and the hero still shows the headline, the primary button and at least one photograph above the fold on a 360×740 phone.

Working rhythm as before: `npm run typecheck` passes after each commit; commit in logical units (concept note, theme additions, hero, portals, this-school and footer, motion and polish); no `npm run build` while the dev server is running.
