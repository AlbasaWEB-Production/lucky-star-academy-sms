# Analytics roadmap

What the whole-school analytics brief maps to, for **Lucky Star Academy** — a
Primary 1–6 school in Yendi with two campuses (Nayilifong, Kpatuya), run by a
head teacher, a small office and a handful of teachers. The brief was written
for large secondary schools and ERPs; the job is to filter it for this school,
build the data model, the entry screens and the policies first, and only then
the charts.

This is a living document: every phase below stops for the owner's go-ahead.
If a phase is declined, it is recorded here and the next approved one proceeds.

---

## The school, as it actually is

- **Levels:** Primary 1–6 only. No Nursery, KG, JHS or SHS. Level breakdowns in
  the brief assume a structure this school does not have.
- **Campuses:** Nayilifong and Kpatuya — already on `classes.campus`.
- **People:** one head, a small office, a handful of teachers. A teacher may
  teach more than one subject; there is no department structure.
- **Finance:** no bursar. Money is handled by the head/office under the **admin**
  role. A separate bursar role is a **proposal** (below), not a build.
- **Term calendar:** three terms per year, already in `public.terms`.
- **Reference data:** fetches, budget lines and capacity are the school's own
  numbers. None is guessed.
- **Role model:** five roles now — `admin`, `teacher`, `student`, `accountant`
  and `schedule_officer`. The last two arrived with their own portals and
  changed nothing on this roadmap except the fee and timetable views noted
  below. Nothing here adds a sixth.

## The two decisions the roadmap has to make up front

### 1. How money is stored

**Pesewas as an integer.** Every amount column is `integer` representing the
smallest unit (Ghana pesewas). Integer arithmetic is exact — no `0.1 + 0.2`
rounding on a sum of fee payments, and the comparison "is the assessed amount
fully covered" is a plain `>=`.

- One formatter, one file: `@/lib/money` exporting `formatCedis(pesewas)`
  (→ `GH₵ 1,234.50`) and `cedisStringToPesewas(text)` (the inverse, for the
  amount input). Every screen, table and chart tooltip goes through it. No
  amount is ever formatted with a bare `toLocaleString()`.
- Input screens accept cedis text and store pesewas; the formatter is the only
  place that divides by 100.
- Documented in `MIGRATION.md` the same phase it lands.

### 2. Which role touches finance

The brief forbids adding a role in this task (it touches `jwt_role`,
`src/proxy.ts`, the auth actions and every policy). So:

- **Finance lives under `admin`.** Fee structures, budgets, expenses and the
  defaulters list are admin-only.
- A **pupil** (and their parent, who shares the login) sees **only their own**
  balance, assessments and receipts.
- A **teacher** sees fee status for the classes they teach — the brief makes
  this conditional on the roadmap approving it. **Approved:** it is genuinely
  useful (a teacher who knows a child has not paid can raise it at home), and
  it is scoped the same way attendance/marks already are — the teacher's own
  classes only. The defaulters list and the incident register stay admin-only.
- A **bursar role** is a proposal with the exact files it would touch (below).

## Not applicable — recorded, not built

| Brief item | Why it does not apply to this school |
| --- | --- |
| GPA | The academic model here is **marks + grade bands** (`dashboard_thresholds.grade_*_min`), not a GPA scale. A pupil has one mark per subject, not a credit-point average. |
| LMS adoption and assignment submissions | There is no LMS. The school records marks directly; there is no submission/deadline pipeline to adopt. |
| Device usage | No devices are tracked; this is not a school-facing metric, and tracking a children's school system should be a deliberate decision. |
| Bus routes and transport | No bus fleet. It is a walking school; there is no transport data or provider. |
| Classroom and lab utilisation heat maps | No timetable of classroom bookings, no labs. `subjects.sessions` is free text ("Mon, Wed"), not periods. **Partly unblocked:** `public.timetable_slots` now models day/period/room for the Schedule Officer portal, so *classroom* utilisation is now computable. Lab utilisation is still not — there is no room register, no capacity and no lab flag, and `timetable_slots.room` is nullable free text rather than a foreign key. |
| Radar charts of teacher evaluation scores | No evaluation/observation process is recorded; and radar charts are not a form the app uses. |

## The phases

Each phase is a migration (or a set of migrations) numbered after
`20260101000300`, plus its read helpers, server actions, screens and charts. No
existing applied migration is edited, no existing column dropped or renamed, and
`database.types.ts` is regenerated with `npm run db:types` after each. `verify.sql`
gets a check and `rls_test.sql` gets cases for every new table in the phase that
adds tables. `npm run typecheck` passes after every commit.

### Phase 1 — Fees and finance (migration `20260101000400`)

**Tables** (all `school_id`, RLS enabled, policies per role, `security_invoker`
views, `security invoker` functions with `set search_path = ''`):

- `fee_structures` — id, school_id, class_id, term_id, description,
  amount_pesewas integer, due_date, timestamps. Unique `(class_id, term_id)`.
- `fee_assessments` — id, school_id, student_id, term_id, fee_structure_id,
  amount_pesewas integer, due_date, created_at. Unique `(student_id, term_id)`;
  regeneration is an upsert, so it is idempotent.
- `fee_payments` — id, school_id, assessment_id, paid_on, amount_pesewas integer,
  method (`cash` | `mobile_money` | `bank`), receipt_number, recorded_by,
  reversed_at, reversal_reason, created_at. **No delete policy.** Reversal sets
  `reversed_at` + `reversal_reason`.
- `budget_lines` — id, school_id, term_id, cost_centre, description,
  planned_amount_pesewas integer, timestamps.
- `expenses` — id, school_id, term_id, cost_centre, description,
  amount_pesewas integer, spent_on, created_at.
- `receipt_counters` — id, school_id, next_number integer. Used by
  `fn_next_receipt_number()` with `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` so numbers are sequential
  per school and never reused. `receipt_counters` is admin-write only.

**Functions** (all `security invoker`, `set search_path = ''`):
- `fn_next_receipt_number()` → the next receipt number for the school.
- `fn_reverse_payment(payment_id, reason)` → sets the reversal fields, validates
  it is not already reversed.

**Cost centre:** a fixed list via a CHECK constraint — `Teaching materials`,
`Administration`, `Facilities`, `Feeding`, `Sports`, `Other`. This is the
school's call in Phase 1; the list is a constraint, not free text, so budget and
expense always line up.

**Screens** (all admin unless noted):
- `/admin/finance/fee-structures` — set an amount, description and due date per
  class and term. Read-only for others.
- `/admin/finance/assessments` — generate assessments for a class and term
  (server action, idempotent upsert), and view/by the term.
- `/admin/finance/payments` — record a payment (assessed-pupil, amount, method);
  the receipt number is assigned by the database.
- `/admin/finance/receipts/[id]` — view or print a receipt (admin, and the pupil
  who owns it).
- `/admin/finance/budget` — budget lines per cost centre and term; `/admin/finance/expenses` — record spend.
- `/student/finance` — **pupil/parent only their own** balance, assessments and
  receipts. Receipts list their own numbers; no other pupil's rows.

**Charts** (admin, on `/admin/finance`; Recharts, explicit heights, a
question-stating title, labelled axes, text alternative):
- Collected vs expected by term, with a target line (line chart, target from
  `fee_collection_target_percent`).
- Outstanding by class as a composition (stacked bars).
- Collection rate + average days to pay — KPI cards with a previous-term
  comparison arrow (real prior term only; otherwise the figure alone).
- Sortable defaulters table (balance, days overdue) — admin-only, `searchParams`
  driven.
- Monthly cash position (net of payments less expenses over months).
- Budget vs actual by cost centre, with variance (grouped bars).

### Phase 2 — Deepen academics (migration `20260101000500`)

**Schema changes:**
- `exam_results.term_id` — add a nullable FK to `terms`. Existing rows are
  backfilled by matching their `created_at` into the term window; rows outside
  any term stay null and read as "no term". No column is dropped or renamed.
- `students.enrolled_at date`, `students.enrolment_status` (`active` |
  `withdrawn` | `completed` | `transferred`), `students.status_date date`.
  Existing pupils default to `active` with `status_date = created_at` — that is
  their true current state, not an invented one.
- `dashboard_thresholds` — a new `pass_mark` key (the grade bands already exist).

**Views** (`security_invoker`): `v_enrolment_trend_by_class_campus`,
`v_retention_dropout`, `v_pass_promotion_rates` (term-scoped via the new
`term_id` + `pass_mark`), `v_class_average_trend`. `v_marks_by_class_subject` is
**left as the snapshot rollup it already is** — it is not extended with `term_id`;
per-term history lives in the four new views. This is a deliberate deviation from
the earlier plan, recorded here and not reclassified after the fact.

**Screens:**
- `/admin/analytics/academics-enrolment` — enrolment trend by class and campus,
  retention/dropout.
- `/admin/analytics/academics-performance` — pass and promotion rates against the
  configured pass mark; class average trend.
- `/admin/progress/[studentId]` — the **pupil progress card**: one child's
  attendance %, marks by subject against the pass mark, fee balance and welfare
  flags on one screen. Admin and that child's teachers; never another child.
- `/student/progress` — the same card for the pupil's own record only.

**Charts:** enrolment trend (multi-class line/series), retention & dropout
(bars), pass/promotion rates (bars against the pass mark line), class average
trend (line), progress card figures.

### Phase 3 — People and teaching operations (migration `20260101000600`)

> **Status: built.** Migration applied; views `v_pupil_teacher_ratio` and
> `v_teacher_attendance_rate` live (admin-only, in-view `jwt_role()` gate);
> read helpers in `src/lib/data/people.ts`; screen `/admin/analytics/people`
> with ratio + attendance charts; RLS suite extended (Section 21, 16 probes).

No new columns. New views and read helpers only:
- `v_pupil_teacher_ratio` — students per class / distinct teachers per class,
  per campus.
- `v_teacher_attendance_rate` — from `teacher_attendance`, bounded by the term
  date window.
- Uncovered subjects is already computed (`subjects.teacher_id IS NULL`).

Screens (admin, and the teacher-relevant ones are largely already there):
- `/admin/analytics/people` — pupil–teacher ratio by class and campus (bars),
  subject load per teacher (existing table), teacher attendance rate over the
  term (line/bars), classes without a teacher for a subject (admin list).

**Not built in this phase (proposals):** professional-development hours,
observation ratings — no tables, no screens, unless the head asks.

### Phase 4 — Admissions and capacity (migration `20260101000700`)

> **Status: built.** Migration applied; `admissions` table + `classes.capacity`
> live; views `v_admissions_funnel`, `v_new_enrolments_by_class_intake` and
> `v_capacity_utilisation` live (admin-only, in-view `jwt_role()` gate); read
> helpers and stage constants in `src/lib/admissions.ts` +
> `src/lib/data/admissions.ts`; actions in `src/lib/actions/admissions.ts`;
> screens `/admin/admissions` and `/admin/analytics/admissions` with the funnel,
> new-enrolments and capacity charts; sidebar entries added; RLS suite extended
> (Section 22, 25 probes).

**Schema changes:**
- `classes.capacity integer` (nullable; null reads as "not set", shown honestly).
- `admissions` table — id, school_id, pupil_name, guardian_name, guardian_phone,
  source, intake_term_id, **class_id**, stage (`enquiry` | `application` | `offer`
  | `enrolled` | `declined`), stage_date, received_on, submitted_on, offered_on,
  enrolled_on, declined_on, created_at. A row's `stage` advances (with
  `stage_date`); it is a lead database, not a per-birth table.
- `class_id` is **not** in the original sketch above but is required so the
  new-enrolments view can say which class an enrolled lead joined. It stays null
  until a lead reaches `enrolled` — an enquiry is not yet in any class. Both
  `intake_term_id` and `class_id` use composite FKs
  (`(id, school_id)`) with `on delete restrict` (school_id is NOT NULL, so
  `set null` is not an option).

**Views** (`security_invoker`): `v_admissions_funnel` (count per current stage;
every stage emitted so the funnel keeps its five-bar shape), 
`v_new_enrolments_by_class_intake`, `v_capacity_utilisation` (capacity null →
utilisation null, a dash — never a fabricated number).

**Screens:**
- `/admin/admissions` — record an admissions lead and advance its stage.
- `/admin/analytics/admissions` — the funnel, new enrolments by class and intake,
  capacity utilisation.

**Charts:** funnel as a **horizontal bar per stage with the conversion % written
beside it** (not a trapezoid), new enrolments by class and intake (bars),
capacity utilisation as enrolled/capacity with a **capacity line** (bar, not a
gauge).

### Phase 5 — Welfare (migration `20260101000800`)

> **Status: built.** Migration applied; `incidents` table and views
> `v_incidents_by_type` + `v_incidents_per_hundred_by_class` live (admin-only,
> in-view `jwt_role()` gate); helpers and constants in `src/lib/incidents.ts` +
> `src/lib/data/welfare.ts`; actions in `src/lib/actions/incidents.ts`; screens
> `/admin/incidents` (register: record, resolve, delete) and `/teacher/incidents`
> (their classes only); welfare flag on both pupil progress cards; charts
> incidents-by-type and per-hundred; RLS suite extended (Section 23, 24 probes).

**Table:** `incidents` — id, school_id, student_id, class_id, date,
incident_type (`lateness` | `truancy` | `fighting` | `bullying` | `property_damage`
| `other`), note (short, factual; the form does not prompt for health, family or
home circumstances), resolved boolean, resolved_on, recorded_by. `class_id` is
denormalised so incident counts need no join for the by-class charts.

**Views** (`security_invoker`): `v_incidents_by_type`, `v_incidents_per_hundred_by_class`.

**Screens:**
- `/admin/incidents` — the **admin-only** incident register (record, resolve).
- `/teacher/incidents` — incidents for the classes the teacher teaches only.
- Welfare flag on the pupil progress card (Phase 2) — an unresolved incident
  surfaces as a flag.

**Charts:** incidents by type (bars), incidents per hundred pupils by class
(bars), progress-card flag.

## RLS and security summary

Every new table gets `school_id`, RLS enabled, and policies in the style of
`20260101000100_rls_policies.sql`. The non-obvious scopes:

| Table / view | admin | teacher | student |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fee_structures` | full | own classes | own class (read-only) |
| `fee_assessments` | full | own classes | own only |
| `fee_payments` | full | own classes | own only; no delete policy ever |
| `budget_lines`, `expenses`, `receipt_counters` | full | none | none |
| `admissions` | full | none | none |
| `incidents` | full | own classes | own only |
| `v_defaulters` | only | none | none |
| `v_admissions_funnel` | only | none | none |
| `v_capacity_utilisation` | only | none | none |
| `v_pupil_fee_summary` | — | — | own only |

**No fabricated data.** No sample fees, seeded payments or demonstration
incidents — ever. Every empty state states what will fill it and which screen
does that. With few rows these charts will be honest and mostly empty at first;
an honest `0`/empty chart is presentable, a plausible invented number is not.

**Protect the children.** A pupil never sees another pupil's marks, attendance,
balance or incidents. The defaulters list and the incident register are
admin-only (teacher sees incidents only for their own classes). Nothing ranks
pupils against each other, and no class's defaulters appear anywhere a pupil can
reach.

**Do not touch** `src/lib/auth/**` or `src/proxy.ts`.

## Proposals left for the owner (not built)

1. **Bursar role** — moving finance out of the admin role. Would touch:
   `jwt_role`, `src/proxy.ts`, the auth actions (`src/lib/actions/auth*`), the
   role layouts and every finance policy. It is deliberately out of scope here.
2. **Professional-development tracking** — a `teacher_pd` table (hours, type,
   date) and an observation rating table, if the head wants staff development
   tracked.
3. **Co-curricular participation** — `clubs` and `club_memberships` tables, if
   the school records clubs. Empty-state copy today says what would fill it.

## How this roadmap maps back to "done means"

- The head dashboard is reorganised into **Academic, People, Finance, Admissions,
  Welfare**, each led by its single most important figure with its trend, then
  expanding to the detail beneath. (Final step, after the five data phases land.)
- Finance works end to end (structures → assessments → payments → receipts →
  reversals); academics show term-scoped enrolment, retention/dropout,
  pass/promotion, class average trend and the pupil progress card; people show
  ratio, load, teacher attendance and uncovered subjects; admissions show the
  funnel, new enrolments and capacity; welfare shows incidents and the progress
  card flag.
- `ANALYTICS-ROADMAP.md` maps every item above to built / deferred-with-what-it-
  needs / not-applicable-with-reason, and **matches what shipped** — a built item
  is not reclassified here after the fact.
